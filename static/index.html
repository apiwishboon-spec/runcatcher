Privacy Policy

Chronopic

Last Updated: 2026-01-31

Chronopic is built on a "Privacy-by-Design" philosophy. We believe that monitoring should never mean surveillance. This policy outlines how we protect the privacy of students and staff while maintaining a quiet study environment.

1. Local-Only Processing (Edge AI)

Our AI does not "watch" you in the traditional sense. All video and audio streams are processed locally in your browser using MediaPipe and Web Audio API. Raw video and audio data never leave your device. The server only receives metadata about alerts, not the live feed.

2. Incident Snapshots & Evidence

The system only captures a snapshot when a specific "Run" or "Noise" threshold is triggered. These snapshots are intended for immediate librarian review and include:

  • Timestamp and Detection Zone
  • Calculated Speed (m/s) and Noise Level (dB)
  • A single image of the triggering event
3. Ephemeral Storage (5-Hour Rule)

We hate digital hoarding. All incident evidence, snapshots, and alert logs are automatically and permanently deleted after 5 hours. We do not maintain long-term archives or behavioral profiles.

4. No Biometrics or Identity Tracking

LibraryRunCatcher does not use facial recognition, gait analysis, or voice identification. It tracks skeletal "blobs" and decibel levels. We see motion and sound, not people.

5. Librarian's Watch (Sync)

Multi-device synchronization uses temporary, randomized room codes. No user accounts, passwords, or personal identifiers are required. Connections are encrypted and terminated when the session ends.

6. Compliance & Ethics

This program is designed to comply with Thailand PDPA, GDPR, and FERPA principles by minimizing data collection and ensuring transparency. It is intended for educational supervision, not punitive surveillance.

Terms of Service

Chronopic

Last Updated: 2026-01-31

By using Chronopic, you agree to these operational terms. This software is a tool for education, not a weapon for surveillance.

1. Authorized Use Case

Chronopic is designed exclusively for libraries, quiet study zones, and educational halls. Use for covert home surveillance, workplace productivity tracking, or punitive monitoring is strictly prohibited and outside the scope of its intended design.

2. Human-in-the-Loop Governance

The AI detections (Speed/Noise) are probabilistic indicators, not absolute facts. Every alert must be reviewed by a human librarian before any disciplinary action is taken. The software is a helper, not a judge.

3. Transparency Requirements

Institutional users are responsible for ensuring that individuals in monitored zones are aware that an automated behavior-assistance system is in use. Transparency builds trust.

4. Data Ownership & Responsibility

Users are responsible for any exported data (PDF reports). Once data is downloaded from the 5-hour ephemeral storage, it becomes the responsibility of the institution to protect that information.

5. No Warranty (As-Is)

LibraryRunCatcher is provided as-is. While we strive for 99% accuracy in motion detection, environmental factors (lighting, echoes) can affect performance. We are not liable for false alerts or missed detections.

Complete User Manual
Getting Started
Step 1

Create a Room

Go to "Librarian's Watch" and click the icon to generate a unique Room Code.

Step 2

Select a Zone

Choose a zone name from the dropdown (e.g., "Reading Area") or add a new one that describes your camera's location.

Step 3

Start Monitoring

Toggle the "Auto" switch or click the button to enable your camera and mic.

Core Features

Live Monitoring

  • Speed Detection: Uses your camera to estimate movement speed (m/s).
  • Noise Detection: Uses your microphone to measure sound in decibels (dB).
  • Streak Counter: Shows consecutive quiet readings — aim for a high streak!
  • Status Badge: Displays "QUIET", "LOUD", or "RUNNING DETECTED".

Librarian's Watch

  • Multi-Device Sync: Connect your phone to receive alerts from the laptop's camera.
  • Haptic Feedback: Your phone will vibrate when an alert is triggered.
  • Emergency SOS: Click SOS to send a full-screen alarm to all synced devices.

Admin Dashboard & Map

  • Interactive Map: Drag and drop camera nodes to build your floor plan.
  • Add Zones: Click "Add Shape" to draw study areas, bookshelves, or corridors on the map.
  • Sync Zones: Click the button in Zone Selection to sync your drawn shapes as zone options.
  • Real-time Alerts: Pulsing red icons show active incidents. Click to see snapshots.
  • Import/Export: Use "Export .lrc" to backup your map. Use "Restore" to load it back after server restarts.
  • Secure Access: Requires a 6-digit PIN generated when you create a room.

Alerts & Evidence

  • Automatic Snapshots: When an alert triggers, a photo is captured instantly.
  • PDF Reports: Download individual or batch reports with timestamps.
  • Auto-Cleanup: All snapshots are automatically deleted after 5 hours for privacy.
  • Clear History: Manually clear alerts with the "Clear" button.
Advanced Settings

CCTV Grid Mode

Enable Grid Detection to divide your camera view into zones (e.g., 2x2, 3x3). Each cell tracks activity independently — useful for multi-monitor CCTV setups.

Export & Restore (.lrc)

Your map layouts are saved in server memory. To prevent loss after restarts, use Export .lrc to save a backup to your computer. Use Restore to reload it anytime.
